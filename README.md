Neon Tide is an original top-down open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked coastal city of Porto Alba: explore four districts on foot and by car, steal and drive anything with wheels, run a four-job story for fixer Dom Paz, and outrun a five-star wanted system with intercepting police — all in plain hand-rolled canvas JavaScript with synthesized sound and no libraries.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD or the arrow keys walk and drive, E enters and leaves cars, the mouse aims and fires, P pauses. Progress (cash and story) saves itself in the browser.

## What's in the game

- **Porto Alba** — a generated coastal city in four districts (downtown, suburbs, the docks, Marina Beach) with avenues, crosswalks, palms, shipping containers and a pier reaching into the ocean.
- **A four-job story** — courier Mari Sol takes work from fixer Dom Paz: a crate run, a stolen Solstice Roadster, singer Vera Lark's timed escape, and the High Tide score, plus respawning cash stashes in free roam.
- **Living traffic and crowds** — drivers loop the avenues, brake and honk; pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and flee gunfire, crashes and speeding cars.
- **A five-star wanted system** — witnessed crimes raise heat, Interceptors spawn to pursue and ram, officers shoot at three stars, and standing still near one fills the bust meter. Lose them and the heat cools.
- **Sound, synthesized live** — engine, sirens, gunfire, horns and cash registers from WebAudio oscillators and noise; there are no audio files and no libraries anywhere in the game.
